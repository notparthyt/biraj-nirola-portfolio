Background

I'm Biraj Nirola, a professional Mobile Thumbnail Designer from India, operating under the creative brand Harshira Visuals. With over 4 years of dedicated experience, I've built a deep understanding of what makes a viewer click — and more importantly, what makes them stay.

My work lives at the intersection of art and strategy. Every thumbnail I design is crafted with purpose: the right colors to trigger emotion, the right composition to guide the eye, and the right energy to match the content. Whether it's the high-octane world of gaming or the authentic vibes of real-life content, I deliver visuals that perform.

Fluent in both Hindi and English, I work seamlessly with creators across India and beyond. My design philosophy is simple — a great thumbnail isn't just eye-catching, it's a promise to the viewer. I help creators keep that promise, every single time.

I'm constantly evolving with the industry — learning new tools, studying top-performing content, and refining my craft. When you work with Harshira Visuals, you're not just getting a thumbnail. You're getting a competitive edge.
